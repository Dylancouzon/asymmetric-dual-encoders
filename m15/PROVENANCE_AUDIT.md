- Explicit source links/paths in `EVIDENCE.md`: 24 named targets, all tracked, including the published reserved aggregate.

## What is outside Git or still incomplete

- **Heavy experiment assets:** `artifacts/`, most `work/`, `edge_data/`, model/teacher weights, encode matrices, populated engine collections, and virtual environments are ignored or externally obtained. Some result records contain hashes/identities rather than the input bytes.
- **Teacher-screen fit list:** the cleaned 337,981-query fit list is not tracked and is absent on this Mac. Its hash is recorded; fit matrices/caches remained on the stopped compute pod. Exact reproduction needs recovery of the list or a verified reconstruction. A hash alone cannot recover it.
- **Million-passage diagnostic:** its sampled document-ID list is local and untracked, and its vectors are outside Git. Recover/preserve the sampled IDs before promising an exact rerun; do not substitute a different sample without labeling it.
- **M20 dataset/vector archive:** the committed archive manifest describes 2,223 files, 142.6 GiB. The separate local target was verified; the object-storage copy remains outstanding. See [M20 status](../m20/STATUS.md). A manifest is not the archive bytes.
- **Historical raw logs:** some were ignored or stayed on other machines. For example, the old fusion-depth log is absent, but the complete curve used by Table 3 is committed in [m12/tier1.json](../m12/tier1.json). Historical engineering observations often use milestone summaries and their named receipts rather than a complete console transcript.
- **External literature:** public primary-source references and committed verification notes are available; ignored `.firecrawl/` extraction scratch is not included.
- **Protected evaluations:** ordinary review must use published aggregates. Reserved-four raw qrels/queries, closed M18 confirmation, and sealed M19 confirmation are not reopened by this inventory or this collaboration task.

**Assessment:** the current manuscript has a reviewable evidence trail in this branch. Exact reruns of every historical or M15 experiment are not self-contained, and full artifact archival is unfinished. Do not promise that a fresh clone contains every input or can regenerate every number offline.
