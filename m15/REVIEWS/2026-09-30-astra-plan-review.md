3. **P1 — E2’s relevance-conditioned subset changes the question and needs an explicit reporting boundary.** [PLAN.md:119](/Users/dylanc/Documents/GitHub/asymetric-dual-encoders/m15/PLAN.md:119) retains all judged positives while removing most competing passages.
