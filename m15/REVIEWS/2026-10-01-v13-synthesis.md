## Judgment

V13 is a good scoped applied research paper. The earlier cost/teacher-first direction underrepresented Constella and has been corrected. Its central question is what selectable query compute buys over frozen document vectors. The strongest measured system result remains the approximately 62-fold encoding gap becoming 2.2-fold including retrieval in the shared binary diagnostic at each tier's own quality target. Hot swapping and the no-transformer path fit that question; neither is an unqualified zero-latency or zero-loading claim.

The controlled query-precision result adds a useful second research finding: keeping query magnitudes restores candidate information with the same document sign codes and encoder. All tiers benefit, and Zero's larger absolute gain has more miss headroom. This is not a new quantizer, a native latency result, or proof of a geometric mechanism. Native precision/cost validation could strengthen the paper; no architectural breakthrough or guaranteed community reception is claimed.
