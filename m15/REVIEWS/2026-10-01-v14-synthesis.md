## Skeptical assessment

The evidence is more than anecdotal: it includes paired controlled interventions, broad quality evaluation, deterministic samples, and a teacher roster. That supports a scoped applied study, not a law about all static query encoders. One controlled counterexample is sufficient to show that alignment alone does not certify ANN settings; it does not establish how common the problem is. Twenty-six checkpoints make the teacher/table result substantial within the tested recipe, without validating the screen for trained Zero/Nano. The native quantization interaction remains workload-dependent; the repeated exhaustive scoring result remains non-native.

Two different stronger claims require different follow-ups. A native precision test at matched quality/cost would strengthen practical guidance. A compact scoring replication in another teacher space would extend the finding beyond Stella, potentially using an existing closed-form table rather than retraining a full student. Those are research choices, not automatic gates for this scoped draft.
