**Verdict line:** "The draft is not statistically or algebraically defensible as written. The
Holm-sensitive BM25 contrast itself is handled correctly, but several surrounding claims contradict
that discipline."

1. **The statistical decision rule is misstated. Hard error.** Section 4 says "Established means the
   registered lower bound excluded the bar". That is false for the paper's own Zero-vs-BM25 result:
   its interval excludes zero, but it is unresolved because p=0.0149 fails the Holm threshold
   0.0083. "Established" therefore requires satisfying the entire registered decision rule. Section 4
   also presents "fixed sequence with Holm control" as one procedure, while the canonical record
   separates the Nano fixed-sequence procedure from the Zero Holm-controlled family.
2. **The CI-excludes-zero-but-fails-Holm case is handled correctly. No finding.**
3. **Two unresolved comparisons are nevertheless called ties. Hard error.** The OpenSearch row says
   "Unresolved, a statistical tie". Section 5.3 calls DBSF and convex0 "a tie in the strict sense
   that neither is established over the other". Failure to establish superiority is not a tie or
   equivalence. The DBSF/convex0 claim is worse: no confidence interval was computed at all. The
   canonical benchmark explicitly prohibits both readings.
4. **The claim that four contrasts are unresolved is not accounted for. Weak claim.** The tables
   contain three: Nano-LEAF clean-4, Zero-BM25, and fused-OpenSearch. DBSF versus convex0 cannot
   silently become the fourth because it is descriptive and has no interval.
5. **Sections 1 and 6 are logically compatible, but the latency range is overgeneralized. Hard
   error in Section 6.** Section 1 concerns avoiding a corpus re-encode; Section 6 concerns
   per-request latency. A small system-level speedup does not negate the operational advantage.
   However the 1.11x to 3.28x range, "depending entirely on index configuration", was measured for
   6 to 10-word queries in the configuration and memory sweeps. The same source reports 5.10x for
   51 to 120-word queries and says the disadvantage is length-dependent. "Entirely" is false and the
   range needs its query-length condition. The abstract repeats the unqualified range.
6. **The "any per-token lookup-table encoder" absorbability theorem is false without a pooling
   condition. Hard error.** Counterexample: under sum pooling q = sum_i e(t_i), an affine map after
   pooling gives Aq + b, while replacing every row by e'(t) = A e(t) + b gives sum_i e'(t_i) =
   Aq + n b, which is not Aq + b for variable-length queries. The result holds under mean pooling,
   or more generally affine pooling whose token coefficients sum to one. Per-token scalar weighting
   also requires weights determined solely by token identity.
7. **The numerical sentence offered as verification contradicts itself. Weak claim.** "rank
   agreement is 1.000 without renormalization and 0.000 with it" does not verify exact absorption.
   A hostile reader can read it as direct disconfirmation.
8. **The teacher-quality result is generalized beyond what eight observations establish. Weak
   claim.** The section title and the contribution overrule the draft's own "counterexample, not a
   law" caution.
9. **The highest-ceiling teacher's rank is described incorrectly. Hard error.** A highest-ceiling
   candidate is first under the ceiling ordering. It was the distilled table that ranked fifth.
10. **BM25 is repeatedly misclassified as a query encoder sharing the dense index. Hard error.**
    BM25 needs its own lexical index and emits no vector into the Stella space. There are three
    dense query encoders plus lexical and fused configurations, not four query encoders sharing one
    dense index.
11. **"65 times faster than either transformer tier" is numerically false. Hard error.**
    7.2511/0.1119 = 64.8x and 6.8400/0.1119 = 61.1x.
12. **The paper claims quantization has no quality trade without measuring recall. Hard error.**
    The cited experiment establishes compression, memory feasibility, and latency. The source
    explicitly defers the quantization comparison on recall to later work.
13. **The "every number traces to a committed result file" assurance is ahead of its own evidence
    process. Weak claim.** All four spot-check batches in the evidence index are still pending.
