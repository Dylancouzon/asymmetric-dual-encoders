1M MS MARCO Passages, Binary Quantization

BM25 on the same passages: 0.66 ms at nDCG@10 0.4895; Zero 1.11 ms at 0.6094 (whole-request timing). Exploratory selection of the registered sweep.

[~0.75 min] On the binary-quantized million-passage MS MARCO index, the sum of separate encoding and search medians put Zero at about 19 times Stella's speed and 2.2 times Nano's, at lower nDCG@10. For this exploratory comparison, I used each encoder's fastest setting within 1% of its own exact score. In the whole-request comparison on those passages, BM25 took 0.66 ms at 0.4895 nDCG@10 and Zero took 1.11 ms at 0.6094, so BM25 saved 0.45 ms and Zero scored 24.5% higher.
