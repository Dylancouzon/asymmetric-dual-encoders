[~1.5 min] I built tonight's passage index on my desktop. It took about five GPU-hours of steady encoding. In real time it took six and a half hours, including a memory spill and a crash. I could go and make tea while that job ran. I'd rather my users didn't have time to do the same. [Point to the grey block.] That's the index: 2.68 million passages, paid for once. [Point to the red blocks.] Every question also goes through Stella before Qdrant can search. A question is much shorter than a passage, so each one is cheap. But they never stop. At a hundred queries a second, I spend a whole index worth of compute on questions about every 27 hours. Over one week, that's 6 more blocks. And that encode sits on the path of every request, roughly 20 milliseconds on this laptop, against a quarter of a millisecond for the search itself. If an agent calls search several times to answer one question, those encodes all sit on the path to the answer. I wanted to make that part cheaper. What else could search the passage vectors I'd already paid to make?
