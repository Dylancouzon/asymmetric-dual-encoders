[Draft notes; Sol pass pending.] A dual encoder scores a document by the inner product of two normalized vectors. The cost has two very different terms. N times c_D is paid once: encoding the 2.7 million Wikipedia passages behind tonight's demo took 11.5 hours on my desktop RTX 3080. Q of t times c_Q is paid on every uncached query, forever: with Stella that is 31 tokens including its instruction prompt, about 19 GFLOPs, 32 ms on a laptop CPU. Agents multiply Q. And if you want a cheaper model, the usual answer is to change both encoders, which means paying N times c_D again and running two indexes during the migration. What if you only change E_Q?
