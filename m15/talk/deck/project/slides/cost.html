Encode the Corpus Once, Pay for Every Query

Index: 2,681,468 NQ passages, Stella fp32, one RTX 3080, 5.3 GPU-hours at a steady 141 passages/s. Request: BEIR NQ, 3,452 queries, one Qdrant Edge shard in-process, M5 Pro CPU, medians; search at hnsw_ef 256, oversampling 4. Exploratory.

[~1.5 min] I built tonight's passage index on my desktop. It took about five GPU-hours of steady encoding. In real time it took six and a half hours, including a memory spill and a crash. I could go and make tea while that job ran. I'd rather my users didn't have time to do the same. [Point to the grey field.] That's 2.68 million passages, and I paid for them once. [Point to the red bars.] Then every search pays again. On this laptop, Stella takes about 20 milliseconds to encode one question. [Point to the orange sliver.] Qdrant then searches all 2.68 million passages in about a quarter of a millisecond. The vector search is the cheap part of the request. If an agent calls search several times to answer one question, those encodes can all sit on the path to the answer. I wanted to make that part cheaper. What else could search the passage vectors I'd already paid to make?
