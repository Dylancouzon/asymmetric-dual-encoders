Zero Trains on a Desktop GPU Against a Frozen Index

one RTX 3080

≈ 20 min to train

Zero: phase 1 16,000 steps × 512, phase 2 2,500 steps InfoNCE, τ 0.02; retrain time is a historical estimate; a new teacher needs 8-12 h of target encoding. All training sources permit commercial derived weights.

[~2 min] Why it matters: the table learns to speak Stella without touching the index. - Goal: rows whose average lands where Stella would put the query. - Start: each row is Stella's encoding of that single token. Rare tokens start with more weight (IDF). - Phase 1, Align (16,000 steps, batches of 512): - Targets: Stella's query vectors, computed once and saved. - Loss part one: point the same way as Stella's vector (1 − cosine). - Loss part two: for labeled queries, match how Stella spreads its scores over the relevant passage and 31 other candidates (KL divergence). - Why both: a vector can look close to Stella's and still rank a different passage first. - Phase 2, Rank (2,500 steps): InfoNCE on 338,076 labeled query-passage pairs. - Push the relevant passage above 32,768 negatives from a frozen bank. - Drop candidates Stella scores close to or above the relevant one: they may answer the question too. - [Formula line] Ê_D, the document side, never changes. - Cost: about 20 minutes on one RTX 3080, once the targets exist. A new teacher needs 8 to 12 hours to compute targets first. - All training sources allow commercial use of the trained weights. Next: I assumed the best retriever would make the best table.
