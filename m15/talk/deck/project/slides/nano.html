[~1.25 min] Order is a reason I'd still want a transformer on the query side. I used the small bge backbone for Nano. The last layer has 384 channels. A linear head fed just that layer can only move within a slice of Stella's space with at most 384 dimensions. Pool layers 12, eight, and four, and the head gets 1,152 features to work with. It still outputs a 1,024-dimensional vector for the existing index. I initialize the head with a ridge fit. Then I train the backbone and head together on squared error between normalized vectors. I alternate three query batches and one passage batch, each with its corresponding Stella target. The query pool had about 3.5 million texts. Qwen3-8B generated 834,000 of them in seven forms. Training sampled evenly across 12 forms from the combined pool. The recipe search ran on the desktop 3080. The final training run took 57.3 A100 hours, about $95. I pay for that fit once.
