[Draft notes; Sol pass pending.] Why does Zero wait on search? The HNSW graph links document vectors, and every encoder walks the same graph. To compare fairly, I take each encoder's own exact top ten as its reference, and count how many the graph search returns at each hnsw_ef. On the paper's 25-model study, lookup tables needed a median of four times the search breadth to match their teacher's recall on FiQA and TREC-COVID, and twice on SCIDOCS. And you can see it coming before you fit anything: take the teacher's own queries, and compare their distance to the nearest document with the typical distance between neighboring documents. Models whose queries sit farther from the documents had larger gaps, correlation minus 0.64. But recall is not relevance: matching the teacher's nDCG loss needed a wider search for only 12 of 25 models on TREC-COVID.
