Zero encodes 51 times faster than Nano, but the whole request is only 2.4 times faster, because search is now 96 percent of Zero's time. And Zero needs the widest search we tested, ef 1024 with eight-times oversampling, and still lands 1.5 percent below its own exact score. Binary quantization keeps one sign bit per dimension, so candidate generation is Hamming distance over 128-byte codes in RAM, then the top os times k candidates are rescored with the fp16 vectors. That is what gives Zero its speed back.
