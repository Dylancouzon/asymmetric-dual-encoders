[~2.0 min] For this chart, I score every candidate against the documents exactly. That way I can see what each encoder does to the ranking before I introduce graph search. The bars are the mean nDCG@10 across 15 BEIR datasets, with each dataset getting the same weight. This was a registered descriptive evaluation. Use Stella as the reference. Nano keeps 90.5% of its score, and Zero keeps 81.4%. Both score higher than the BM25 baseline on this average. If you run search for one of those workloads, the average is a poor substitute for your own row. Quora is a duplicate-question task, where a query can look a lot like the question it should find. Zero keeps 95% of Stella's score there. On TREC-COVID and FiQA it keeps about two thirds. The cheaper encoder can cross your relevance floor on one workload and fall short on another. I'd use the exact numbers to decide which encoders deserve a place in the latency test. Once I put the graph back into the request, a second question comes up.
