The Best Retriever Made the Weakest Table

Ten registered models ranked by mean nDCG@10 over six BEIR datasets, exact search: the model itself, then its fitted lookup table (gte-large-en-v1.5 0.597 → 0.246; Stella 0.575 → 0.397). Rank correlation across all 26 models: +0.09.

[~1.25 min] Why it matters: you cannot pick your teacher by its leaderboard score. - I fitted a quick lookup table (a few minutes on one A100) to 10 strong models and tested each on its own index. - [Red line] gte-large was 1st as a retriever and 10th as a table: 0.597 down to 0.246. - [White line] Stella was 2nd as a retriever and 1st as a table. That is why Constella uses Stella. - Across all 26 models I tried, the two rankings barely relate (rank correlation +0.09). - Lesson: if you might want a cheap query encoder later, test it on a small index before you encode the whole corpus. Next: even with the best teacher, a lookup table has limits you should know.
