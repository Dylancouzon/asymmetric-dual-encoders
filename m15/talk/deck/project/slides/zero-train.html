[Draft notes; Sol pass pending.] Zero is trained in two phases, and it all fits on a 10-gigabyte desktop card. Each row starts as Stella's own encoding of that token, and each token weight starts at its IDF. Phase one, 16,000 steps of 512: match Stella's query vector with a cosine loss, and match Stella's score distribution over one positive and 31 distractors with a KL term at temperature 0.02. Phase two, 2,500 steps: rank the positive above 32,768 negatives from a bank of stored document vectors, masking known positives and any negative Stella scores above the positive minus 0.02, so we don't punish false negatives. Sources: Amazon ESCI, FEVER, HotpotQA, SQuAD, Mr. TyDi, NQ Open and TriviaQA. Then fold the weights into the rows and store int8 with one scale per row. With targets already computed, a retrain takes about 20 minutes. All training sources permit commercial derived weights.
