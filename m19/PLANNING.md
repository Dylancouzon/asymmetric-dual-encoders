# M19 execution plan

Authority is `instructions-m19.md` as committed at `cfd81f4a79a7683ebdf1d33606e5e7014fb90eed`.
Execution follows its ten ordered stages without adding optimizer, teacher, architecture, corpus or
fusion arms.

The first transition is deliberately narrow: install a deny-boundary for protected paths, bind
the M18 manifests and inherited local bytes, and commit the prospective constants. Term inventory
may then inspect the pinned, redacted M18 corpus and released tokenizer, but not M18 protocol data.
Queries are authored and split only after the term roster is frozen. Candidate construction and
all metric rules precede any quality read. Confirmation remains a separately claimed, resumable
one-shot transaction.

Every consequential transition gets a bounded, read-only review. Review prompts must enumerate
admitted files, forbid recursive search and repeat the reserved and M18-confirmation exclusions.
Only one Astra reviewer runs at a time.
