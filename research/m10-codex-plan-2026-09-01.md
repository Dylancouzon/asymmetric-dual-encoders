# Codex adversarial review of the M10 plan — pass 1 (gpt-5.6-terra, read-only, high effort, 2026-09-01)

Verbatim findings. The reviewer read only the files named in the brief (read-exclusion audited in the full log, which is a gitignored `.log` beside this file). Dispositions: `m10/PLANNING.md` §8.

1. **BLOCKER — M9 six-set close-out before M10 lock.** (a) M10.0 reads per-dataset six-set rows, then locks screens/recipe. (b) Those rows can steer forms, mix, loss, and selection surface; re-registering the final transaction afterward is development on the final six. (c) Freeze all M10 design and final-run protocol before the close-out, or defer close-out until M10 is frozen.

2. **BLOCKER — Reserved fingerprint pass is an unspent held-out access.** (a) Decision 5 opens reserved documents to create persistent exact hashes and MinHash sketches. (b) This contradicts M9’s rule that creating them opens reserved corpora; retained sketches permit membership/dictionary attacks and reveal benchmark structure. “Hash-only” is not blindness. (c) Exclude FineWeb documents, or use a separately governed sealed service that emits only train-row removal decisions and aggregate counts, retains no fingerprints, and is explicitly declared a spent reserved access.

3. **BLOCKER — CQA-2 cannot validate the claimed six-set coverage.** (a) Two StackExchange slices become the primary recipe selector for scientific, biomedical, finance, argument, paper-title, and COVID claims. (b) It rewards forum-style synthesis and offers no qrel-bearing evidence on most target forms; FORMS-12 is teacher agreement on generator-made queries, not retrieval quality. (c) Pre-register a licensed, decontaminated multi-domain qrel screen spanning the intended forms; keep CQA as one component, not the selector.

4. **MAJOR — Arm 5 does not falsify “coverage, not capacity.”** (a) It compares the M9 query pool to the full M10 pool under a changed optimizer, 50/50 document mix, warm start, and likely different effective sampling. (b) A win shows this package helps CQA/FORMS, not that query-form coverage caused M9’s miss or that capacity does not bind. A null is also underpowered evidence, yet stops M10. (c) Add orthogonal arms: real/PAQ versus synthetic, form-balanced versus volume-matched, and a final-dose capacity comparison; specify equivalence and stop rules now.

5. **MAJOR — The document mix repeats an unresolved M9 result.** (a) M10 makes examples 50/50 query/document, while M9’s equal-budget document-mix screen was negative and selected query-only. (b) M9’s build observation supports documents as added breadth, not half of a fixed budget; M10 has no mix arm. (c) Include 100/0, 75/25, and 50/50 at matched query presentations, with a precommitted choice rule.

6. **MAJOR — Batch pilot is confounded and explicitly previously rejected.** (a) BS32 versus BS128 is equal-wall-clock at one-tenth dose. (b) It changes examples seen, optimizer updates, warmup fraction, and schedule progression simultaneously; `m9/EXPLORED.md` records this exact objection. (c) Hold examples/tokens and cycle position fixed, report throughput separately, and run at a dose where the comparison can affect the build.

7. **MAJOR — Phase-2 KL is not decision-ready.** (a) τ=0.02, λ=1, top-64 plus random-64 from a fixed 1M bank are asserted without a calibration arm. (b) At that temperature the distribution can collapse onto a few teacher-near ties; the bank is neither the target corpora nor independently validated. A 5M-example screen can miss a later ranking benefit. (c) Pre-lock teacher entropy/margin diagnostics, seed-document exclusion, fixed negative refresh policy, and a phase-2-only continuation evaluated at matched full-dose checkpoints.

8. **MAJOR — Rank probe cannot support the proposed action.** (a) PCA reconstruction of teacher query vectors at 384 dimensions governs a linear-versus-MLP head decision. (b) Reconstruction retention is not ranking retention against the frozen document index; an MLP does not establish that the ≤35M student is sufficient and cannot use the existing fastembed path. The probe result and 95–97% action are still TBD. (c) Require projected-query retrieval retention on the actual screen bank and a serving-parity plan before making any head decision; otherwise keep the linear head and report the probe descriptively.

9. **MAJOR — FineWeb licensing/provenance is internally contradictory.** (a) M10 calls FineWeb conditionally usable as a seed, while the licensing record says FineWeb/C4 are not approved seed corpora; planning also calls it already approved. Synthetic outputs are called “ours.” (b) ODC-By wrapper terms and an Apache generator do not establish page rights, output provenance, or commercial redistribution rights. (c) Restrict seeds to affirmatively licensed sources until an owner-approved source-level review; pin generator revision/terms, prompts, seed IDs, filters, and attribution, and do not call outputs owned or redistributable.

10. **MAJOR — Synthetic and PAQ contamination controls are insufficiently specified.** (a) Per-query provenance and near-duplicate filtering are promised, but no thresholds, corpus split, seed-output exclusion, or PAQ revision/sampling strata are locked. (b) Generated queries can copy seed text or benchmark-like content; PAQ sampling can overconcentrate Wikipedia factoids and reinforce the NQ result mistaken for capacity. (c) Lock the manifest, dedupe thresholds, seed/document holdouts, per-form/domain quotas, PAQ revision and attribution before target encoding.

11. **MAJOR — Screen inference is multiplicity- and seed-blind.** (a) Six arms, checkpoint watching, CQA-2 and FORMS-12 decisions use inherited pairwise bootstrap rules and one shipping seed. (b) The lower bound does not control recipe-selection multiplicity or training-seed variance; M8 estimates chain variation around 0.0015. (c) Pre-register familywise selection control and at least two confirmation seeds for the winning recipe, or sharply limit the claim to one fixed training realization.

12. **MAJOR — Cost and feasibility arithmetic conflicts.** (a) The plan cites 60–80 A100-hours, 1.5 days for build, 5–6 cloud days, 3–5 RTX days, and hosted generation costs inconsistent with the stated ~1.08B prompt/output-token estimate. (b) The Qwen3-8B/vLLM throughput claim is unmeasured on a 10GB 3080, and M9’s mixed measured rate was 18,984 tok/s, not the 25,970 used in planning. (c) Gate scale-up on per-form smoke measurements and publish a single conservative wall-clock/token/GPU-hour budget.

13. **MAJOR — “Beat LEAF” is not isolated.** (a) C2 compares stella-document+nano-query against arctic-document+LEAF-query. (b) It is a whole-system comparison with different document towers, index sizes, teacher contamination, and encode cost; MongoDB can correctly reject it as evidence that nano or the asymmetric recipe beats LEAF. (c) State only the system-level claim, retain matched costs and per-teacher retention, and add a same-document-tower query-path ablation if any stronger claim is desired.

14. **MINOR — The 35M cap is a product choice masquerading as a scientific conclusion.** (a) The plan says a 109M probe may demonstrate a capacity cap but still refuses a larger student. (b) That leaves the aim on the table without quantifying the quality/latency tradeoff. (c) Precommit a conditional >35M frontier point if the capacity probe clears its threshold; label it a separate product tier, not a replacement for nano.

15. **MINOR — Final conditional can leave the aim claim without the reserved check.** (a) Reserved evaluation runs only if C1 passes, while C2 may pass when C1 fails. (b) The plan could make its LEAF claim with no reserved robustness row. (c) Make the descriptive reserved batch conditional on either claimed contrast, with its zero-alpha status unchanged.

Verdict: not decision-grade. The single change with the highest combined chance of reaching the aim and surviving MongoDB/Snowflake review is to pre-register, before any M9 six-set output, a licensed and decontaminated multi-domain qrel-bearing coverage screen that replaces CQA-2/FORMS-12 as the M10 selector; this tests the thesis on the distributions M10 claims to fix without tuning on the six.
