## New failures

- **BLOCKER — COV is not protected from M10 training data.** Data filtering protects “six + dev + reserved + LoTTE” ([instructions-m10.md:137](/Users/dylanc/Documents/GitHub/asymetric-dual-encoders/instructions-m10.md:137)), not COV. COV admission only screens COV against the six/reserved ([instructions-m10.md:205](/Users/dylanc/Documents/GitHub/asymetric-dual-encoders/instructions-m10.md:205)). Add every admitted COV corpus, queries, and documents to the protected index before PAQ/synthetic construction; publish removals before COV scoring.

- **BLOCKER — M9 close-out can consume the sole reserved batch.** M9’s existing final lock condition is `if C1 passes then execute`; the new order runs that close-out before M10’s final. If M9 C1 passes, its reserved conditional spends the access, leaving M10 unable to honor its C1-or-C2 reserved promise. Amend the M9 close-out into an expressly six-only transaction, or defer it until after M10.

- **BLOCKER — Family B’s stated design is mathematically impossible.** Resolve the equal-total versus matched-query choice. For the requested decomposition, fix query presentations and permit additional document examples/tokens; report that cost. Do not claim equal-example control.

- **MAJOR — Family A cannot test, or stop on, the coverage thesis.** A3–A1 conflates 3M extra examples with new forms; A3–A2 is not volume-matched. “Beat by the MDE with lower bound >0” also lacks a defined inequality. Add a unique-text-volume-matched same-form control and require corrected lower bound > MDE; otherwise call the result an A3-package effect and remove the stop rule.

- **MAJOR — COV can collapse to a narrow, overweighted surface.** Minimum four survivors has no health/legal/long-technical floor, while six BRIGHT slices can dominate an equal-*component* macro. Require domain-family floors and equal family weighting, not equal slice weighting.

- **MAJOR — COV candidate eligibility is unestablished.** MedicalQARetrieval, BRIGHT, and LegalBench are all “CC BY 4.0 to verify” ([instructions-m10.md:209](/Users/dylanc/Documents/GitHub/asymetric-dual-encoders/instructions-m10.md:209)). LegalBench entries have no pinned corpus, split, qrels, or retrieval metric in the mandate. Freeze exact revisions and prove each is an open retrieval task with corpus/qrels before naming it COV.

- **MAJOR — COV cannot carry the claimed coverage conclusion.** The mandate admits it lacks scientific claims, paper titles, and argument retrieval ([instructions-m10.md:213](/Users/dylanc/Documents/GitHub/asymetric-dual-encoders/instructions-m10.md:213)), yet family A decides “whether coverage explains the miss.” It can only support coverage on admitted COV domains. Either add licensed qrel surfaces for the missing forms or narrow the conclusion and stop rule.

- **MAJOR — COV contamination exclusions omit binding sources.** The COV rule omits NutritionFacts mirrors and Reddit finance, despite the licensing map identifying them as NFCorpus and FiQA contamination sources. Add both explicitly to corpus exclusions and provenance checks.

- **MAJOR — τ cannot be selected when required.** τ and its entropy table must be recorded at M10.0-e ([instructions-m10.md:161](/Users/dylanc/Documents/GitHub/asymetric-dual-encoders/instructions-m10.md:161)), but the 10,000 “training queries,” bank, and synthetic data are created only in M10.1. Lock the deterministic rule at M10.0; execute it after the immutable data manifest and before any arm, with source mix, seed, tie-break, and no-in-range fallback fixed.

- **MAJOR — confirmation/multiplicity is still ill-defined.** Six “decisions” contain more than six contrasts, including three-way A and B selections. Specify all tested contrasts, familywise adjustment across them, and confirmation runs that include each selected option and its default comparator.

- **MAJOR — dose/cost plan remains infeasible as written.** Beyond 3.8B versus 4.2B, the planning claim that brute-force mining `4.5M × 1M × 1024` takes 10–20 minutes is off by orders of magnitude: that is roughly 9.2e15 scalar FLOPs. The stated screen budget also cannot be about one box-day: nine default-dose 5M screens plus two confirmations are roughly 4.6B tokens, about 2.8 days at 18,984 tok/s before overhead. Replace this with measured mining and batch-32 throughput gates, or register an approximate-mining method and its audit.

- **MINOR — rank-probe documentation is internally wrong.** The docstring says it repeats in a 512d head, but `HEADS` contains 1024/768/256. Correct the description. Also remove `m10/STATUS.md`’s nonlinear-head dry-run instruction: it contradicts the mandate’s fixed-linear decision.

Verdict: not decision-grade. The six-set ordering itself is repaired, but the rewrite introduces a reserved-access collision, leaves COV train-contaminable and under-specified, makes the mix comparison impossible, and relies on invalid data and cost arithmetic; those prevent a defensible recipe lock.
