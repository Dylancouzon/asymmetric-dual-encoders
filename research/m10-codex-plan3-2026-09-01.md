11. **MAJOR cost/mining — does not fully land.** The mining smoke/HNSW fallback lands, but the revised confirmation budget does not; see new arithmetic failure below.

12. **MINOR rank-probe/nonlinear status conflict — lands.** The mandate says “the head stays linear whatever it says”; the status no longer directs a nonlinear-head run.

### New failures

- **MAJOR — A2 cannot meet its locked corpus size.** A2 requires “M9 pool + PAQ to **4.5M** unique texts,” while PAQ is capped at 4.0M. `463K + 4.0M = 4.463M`, not 4.5M; PLANNING also calls A2’s PAQ increment 3.0M. **Fix:** lock exact counts consistently—e.g. make A2 and A3 both 4.463M, or explicitly raise and approve the PAQ cap to 4.037M.

- **MAJOR — LoTTE read #1 has no candidate or defined veto.** M10.2 runs individual screen arms and confirmations, then reads LoTTE on “selected recipe vs anchor”; it never mandates a combined selected-recipe checkpoint before that one-time read, nor a statistic/outcome that constitutes veto. **Fix:** preregister one synthesized screen-dose selected-recipe run, exact LoTTE test/statistic/veto action, then run it before opening LoTTE.

- **MAJOR — execution status contradicts the mandate’s COV floor.** `m10/STATUS.md` says “four **survivors** minimum”; the mandate requires “four **families**.” Four components from two families could wrongly proceed. **Fix:** change STATUS to four admitted families and make the ledger check enforce it.

- **MAJOR — confirmation-cost ceiling is false after the Family-B redesign.** PLANNING says worst-case confirmations are `≤2.5B` tokens. If 50/50 wins B, its two confirmation seeds cost about 0.994B tokens and the default’s two cost 0.418B; adding two ordinary selected decisions costs about 1.672B: about **3.08B**, not 2.5B. **Fix:** recompute the worst-case budget using each B arm’s actual token load and revise the box schedule/cost gate.

Verdict: **not decision-grade.** Most pass-2 repairs land, but the A stop rule remains weaker than specified, A2’s locked volume is unfillable, the sole LoTTE veto is not executable as written, STATUS can admit an invalid COV surface, and the confirmation budget is materially understated.
