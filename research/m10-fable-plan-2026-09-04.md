## Verdict

> **not lock-ready.** The re-pricing direction is right and the machinery cuts are mostly right, but
> four things must change before the text becomes permanent: A1's cited evidence is about a
> different loss class than the one it cuts; A3 specifies two incompatible multiplicity procedures
> and quietly halves the alpha the avg-6 release claim had; A4's tripwire will very likely fire and
> its remedy is arithmetically impotent; and the whole cost/box story rests on a hardware bound the
> M9 pipeline achieved ~15% of.

## BLOCKERS

**B1. A1 cuts the ranking-aware class on evidence about intermediate-layer distillation, not
ranking losses.** §Amendment A1 row and §Recipe "Objective [family D]": "LEAF's Appendix B added
distillation terms to plain regression and found no improvement." Appendix B of arXiv 2509.12539
adds the **MiniLM, TinyBERT and DistilBERT** losses (hidden-state / attention-matrix KD) to the ℓ₂
loss and gets 54.9 / 53.7 / 55.3 vs 60.7 — intermediate-layer KD hurting, not "a KL over teacher
scores or InfoNCE adds nothing". None of the five cited systems tested a ranking term *on top of*
regression and reported a null; EmbedDistill's ablation is in the opposite direction (score
distillation → +doc embeddings → +L2 query matching → +synthetic queries, each additive). So the
class is cut on mis-generalised evidence, and M9 finding #4 ("phase 2 must be specified at lock or
it does not exist") is recreated: a flat-below-bar plateau again has no registered response except
"stop, report". Fix: correct the sentence (it will be quoted in the paper); and either register one
bank-free ranking-aware arm or register a *non-ranking* plateau response.

**B2. A3 names two incompatible procedures and weakens the avg-6 release claim.** Holm is
*p-value-ordered*; a fixed sequence is a *gatekeeping* procedure. The text lets a future session
claim either after seeing the numbers. Under Holm-4 the smallest-p conjunct needs α/4 = 0.00625
one-sided (z 2.50) where the old two-conjunct design needed 0.0125 (z 2.24): C1a's bound widens
~11% for the same data. And clean-4 is structurally noisier: trec-covid has **n = 50** queries and
carries 25% of the clean-4 macro. Paired bootstrap on `perquery.json` (bge-small vs leaf-ir-asym as
a proxy): point-to-lower-bound width **0.0101 on all-6, 0.0142 on clean-4**. Pass points: C1a 0.514
(89.5% of ceiling), C2a 0.526 (91.5%), C1b 0.519 (92.0%), **C2b 0.537 (95.3%)** — LEAF-level
retention on the headline partition. Fix: pick fixed-sequence gatekeeping (each at the full 0.025,
registered order) — it adds clean-4 at zero alpha cost to avg-6 — and delete "Holm"; or keep Holm
and delete "sequence fixed". Disclose the n=50 weighting.

**B3. A4's 0.010 tripwire is a coin flip and its remedy cannot work.** One-sided 0.025/11 is z =
**2.84**, not 2.96 (that was the 16-contrast value). With the admitted surface (MedicalQA 2,048;
BRIGHT 6×~100; CorporateLobbying 340; CCQA 396; equal family weights) and paired per-query
nDCG-difference SDs from `perquery.json` (0.07–0.16 for near-identical systems, 0.14–0.27 for
unrelated ones), the macro SE is **0.0033–0.0048** and the distance **0.009–0.0135** — straddling
0.010. "Enlarge the surface or cut contrasts" is unspecified and, for the second half, impotent:
11→5 contrasts shrinks the distance 9%; halving it needs 4× the queries, and only LEDGER (118,048
questions) is that big. Fix: register the remedy now. Also `MDE = max(0.0056, distance)` makes
"point ≥ MDE" redundant with "lower bound > 0" — the MDE then expresses what is detectable, not
what matters. Say so or restore a substantive MDE.

## MAJOR

**M1. The harvested-form → clean-4 mapping is asserted from a pool that contains no scientific
text.** The 6.15M pool is Wikipedia (hotpotqa/squad/nq/triviaqa/mrtydi) plus ESCI products. So
"paper-title" harvested = Wikipedia article titles and product names, not 10–15-word technical
titles; "scientific claim" = Wikipedia lead sentences ("X is a Y"), not scifact-style claims;
"consumer-health question" has *no* source (MedQuAD is COV and protected); "factoid question" from
`?`-terminated Wikipedia sentences will have a tiny yield. Six forms × ~250K = 1.5M is unmeasured,
and "three of the four clean-4 datasets fall in the harvested half" is the motivated half of A2.
Fix: measure harvest yields on day one before quotas lock; register the fallback (a harvested form
under 100K post-dedup reverts to generation); add a real scientific source; re-word the mapping as
a hypothesis A3−A2 tests.

**M2. A6 relocates the transfer problem rather than fixing it; the F/C rules pre-decide MiniLM and
no warm start.** F's rule is "bge-small wins only if *resolved*" (point ≥ ~0.010 at 5M examples);
M9's same contrast was −0.0026 unresolved. So F defaults to MiniLM by construction, which skips C
(the M9 candidate's warm start is bge-small-only). Every later family then screens on a student
chosen by default at 2.5% of the dose, where deeper models are disadvantaged. The owner said 33M
*is* acceptable, so the tie-break to the cheaper student is a product preference, not evidence —
label it, and give F the dose its consequence deserves.

**M3. The benchmark is read past what it supports.** (i) "Past bs512 the free regime ends" is
wrong: tok/s *falls* from ~149K (bs128) to ~66K (bs512). A FLOP-bound card plateaus; it does not
drop 2.3×. That is memory pressure on 10 GB — the allocator-thrash signature CLAUDE.md warns about.
Consequence: an A100 (80 GB) is *not* launch-bound at bs128+, so family E's "1.8× cost" and the
cloud build line are device-dependent. (ii) The one shape the build will actually run — an
all-document bucket (32,256) — was never timed; 25% of steps are that shape at 2× the tokens of the
32×128 row. (iii) 60 steps, no sustained-clock effect. (iv) M9's realised **18,984 tok/s at 8,192
tok/step** is ~13–20% of the bench's hardware rate at comparable tokens/step — the pipeline the M10
trainer is being ported *from*. Until the real-data re-measure lands, price the build at 745 ×
(pipeline efficiency) and state the range: at M9's efficiency the box build is 300+ h, not 75, and a
screen arm is 6–9 h, not 1.9. Fix: re-run with (32,256), (64,64), 600 steps, real tokenized data,
`num_alloc_retries` logged; carry two cost columns until then.

**M4. A1's reopening condition is not checkable when it matters.** The release bar lives on the six,
read once at M10.4; during the build only COV/DEV exist. Define it on the dev→six calibration
(forecast, named) or explicitly on the post-final six number.

**M5. The protocol files contradict the amendments.** `m10/LEDGER.md` §1 still requires the
seed-rank field and the bank/mining audit, both struck by A1; §0 calls the resolution number
"descriptive" on one line and "sizes the screen" on another. `m10/EXPLORED.md`: ConsumerContractsQA
"never" vs the mandate's re-admission; "COV resolution check as a screen-cutting rule … never" vs
A4. `m10/COV_CANDIDATES.md` row 15 still says refused. §Surfaces FORMS-12 still scores over "the 1M
bank" — the bank A1 deleted (cheap fix: a 1M-document *evaluation* sample of already-encoded pool
vectors, no mining).

**M6. A7 reverses a verbatim owner ruling under a general grant, and the box window is
oversubscribed.** "M10 won't be done on a 3080" is reinterpreted as "the dose isn't set by the box".
Decision 10 probably covers it, but list A7 separately for Dylan to strike, since it is the only
amendment that contradicts his words. Ten STATUS items in 72 h, and the real screen cannot start in
it because the anchor needs generated data. Order the window and drop the rest.

**M7. Screen alpha is calibrated as if screen decisions were publishable claims.** Bonferroni
0.025/11 one-sided on ~3K COV queries at 5M examples cannot resolve effects of 0.003–0.008 — the
size M9 actually observed for student, mix and seed. Eight of eleven contrasts are pre-destined to
"revert to default", so ~25 GPU-h of arms plus up to 17 GPU-h of confirmations mostly relabel
defaults the plan already holds. Over-conservatism in an *internal* selection costs model quality,
not credibility.

**M8. Form imbalance in the build corpus.** 463K real + 1.0M PAQ = ~37% of query texts are factoid
— the form M9 already retains at 93.8%. Uniform-by-example sampling spends 37% of the query gradient
there. Register form-balanced sampling (or cap PAQ at the other forms' ~250K). Zero cost.

## MINOR

- e5-small-v2 and gte-small are not in `results/perquery.json`; they are FINAL_MATRIX rows needing
  fresh COV encodes, so "frozen" is the wrong word.
- The resolution distance is measured on two *unrelated* models; screen arms share init and data, so
  their paired SD is smaller. Direction is conservative, which feeds M7. A seed pair of the anchor
  is the right noise floor (2 GPU-h) but is a COV read before the lock — note the trade-off.
- A5's screen-dose "replication band" does not bound the full-dose seed range; label it descriptive
  in the report template now.
- Pre-screen on DEV-6 then screen on COV means B/C/E/G are looked at twice; count the DEV-6 reads
  and label the defaults it set.
- Hosted open-weights inference of the pinned revision for ~1.0M queries is ≈$20–60 and needs no
  A100 setup or idle instance; it is the fallback where it should be the default.
- D-NORM is worth running but note the mechanism: on unit vectors ∇‖t−s‖ has unit magnitude per
  example, so poorly-fit (under-covered) forms get *less* relative gradient than under squared L2 —
  the direction of its effect under a coverage thesis is not obvious.
- `m9src/nano.py`'s docstring says "→ L2 normalize" but `forward` returns the unnormalized head;
  normalization happens in the trainer loop. The "closed by algebra" claim is correct because of the
  loop, so pin it to `longrun.py` ~779, not the module.

## Improvement ideas, as given, ranked by the reviewer

1. **`torch.compile(mode="reduce-overhead")` / CUDA graphs on fixed length buckets.** Expect 1.5–3×
   at bs32 → build 75 h → 25–50 h, and most of family E's cost argument disappears. Cost: 0 GPU-h,
   ~2–4 h engineering, one bench row to prove it.
2. **D-COV: document-covariance-weighted regression** as a second family-D arm.
   `L = (s−t)ᵀΣ(s−t)`, Σ the covariance of the pool's stella document vectors. Linear, bank-free,
   fastembed-safe, and *not* absorbable: under a capacity limit (the rank bottleneck) weighted least
   squares selects a different subspace — the one preserving document-discriminative variance, which
   is what nDCG rewards and plain L2 ignores. Directly targets the in-distribution gap. ≈2–4 GPU-h.
3. **arXiv metadata (CC0) as a real scientific source** for paper titles and abstract-sentence
   claims: ~2.4M real titles, permissively licensed, a different source family from S2ORC/PubMed;
   every document through the existing screens. Fills the exact gap the mandate concedes, on the
   headline partition. CPU harvest + ~500K stella encodes ≈ 5 min on the box; one licence check.
4. **Give family F the dose its consequence deserves and widen it.** bge-small and MiniLM-L6 at 20M
   examples (≈7.5 GPU-h each) read as a *curve*; add MiniLM-L12-v2 (33M) and granite-embedding-30m
   at 5M. The Spearman≈0 lesson says tower quality does not predict distillability; two arms is a
   thin screen for the most consequential choice. ≈19 GPU-h total.
5. **Form-balanced sampling** (M8). 0 GPU-h.
6. **A coverage-consistent registered phase 2 (the A1 replacement).** Record per-form retention at
   each cycle end; if the plateau rule fires below the release-bar forecast, one registered top-up
   cycle runs with the bottom-two forms at 2× presentation weight. No new data or machinery; costs
   one extension cycle only if triggered. This is what M9 finding #4 asked for, in the plan's own
   thesis.
7. **Hosted generation as default.** −$15–50, −1 day.
8. **The thing nobody has said: the in-distribution ceiling.** M9 retained **93.8% on NQ with
   NQ-like data** — coverage explains the 50–71%, not the 94%. LEAF reaches 97.7% overall. C2b needs
   ~95.3% on clean-4, and even C2a needs ~91.5%. Nothing in the plan is aimed at 94→98 on *covered*
   forms except width (G) and the optimizer regime (E, cycles); the 400M/1024-d teacher is outside
   the literature. Track nq-250k retention as a named diagnostic on every arm, and let D-COV be the
   arm that attacks it.
9. **Screen alpha reform** (B3/M7): frees ~15–20 GPU-h of confirmations that would confirm nothing
   and lets evidence rather than defaults choose.
