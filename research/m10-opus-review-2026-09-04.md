# Adversarial review — M10 plan, final pass before execution

No excluded path was opened.

## Verdict

Three things stop execution as written: **M10.0-e cannot be filled from anything that exists at M10.0-e**, the **745 examples/s that re-priced the whole milestone is not in the artifact it cites** (measured: 718/702/596, i.e. 81.3 h not 75 h, and 2.2× not 1.8× for family E), and the **gatekeeping order tests the non-headline partition first**, so the plan's own stress table contains a scenario where nano clears the release conjunct decision 11 requires and is forbidden from testing it. Two further defects make registered rules inert: the kill rule cannot fire before the build ends, and "the plateau rule" is never defined. The statistics, the G-MLP algebra and the parity work are sound.

## BLOCKER

**1. `instructions-m10.md:686-687`, `results/m10_rate_bench_box.json:34-36` — the central measured number does not exist.**
Quote (mandate §Compute): `| batch 32, one padded chunk | **745** | **75 h** |` and `| batch 128, 75/25 | 1,331 | 42 h |`.
The artifact's pass-2 bs32 single-chunk rows are **718 / 702 / 596 ex/s**; its own summary block gives `"bs32_gpu_hours": 81.3, "bs128_gpu_hours": 36.6, "bs128_is_cheaper_by": "2.2x"`. `PLANNING.md:418` says the same. **745 and 1,331 appear in no artifact** — they are pass-1-era figures that survived the pass-2 re-run into the mandate (×4), `m10/STATUS.md:15,43` and `CLAUDE.md:25`. Consequences a rule reads: the build line is understated 8% (75 h vs 81.3 h); the screen-arm cost (`:524`, "≈1.9 GPU-hours") is understated; and **family E's registered rule** (`:541`) weighs a bs32 win against "its **1.8×** build cost" when the measured ratio is **2.2×** — the mandate contradicts both PLANNING §11 and the JSON in the same repo. Fix: replace all six occurrences with 683 ex/s effective / 81.3 h (bs32) and 1,518 / 36.6 h (bs128), or state the per-bucket rates and stop quoting a single blended figure.

**2. `m10/LEDGER.md:7-16` vs `instructions-m10.md:276-285` — M10.0-e is circular; it is the first step that cannot be performed from the text alone.**
§0 is headed "fill at M10.0-e, **before any arm**" and requires (`:13`) "A2, A3 and A4 post-screen unique-text counts (identical) and corpus hashes" — these exist only after M10.1's harvest, generation, decontamination and manifest, which the stage plan puts *after* M10.0-e. §0 also requires (`:11`) "generation requests/s per form; the billed $/h; the re-derived PLANNING §6" — the generation smoke and the billed price require a cloud instance that `m10/STATUS.md` ("Then, in order") schedules *after* the lock. Three of §0's fields are unobtainable at the stage that must contain them. Fix: split §0 into **§0a** (arms, order, doses, seeds, surfaces, the fourteen contrasts, MDE/α, confirmation design, outcome→action maps — everything that must precede the data, pushed at M10.0-e) and **§0b** (matched corpus counts and hashes, measured rates, billed price, `max_extension_cycles`), pushed at the close of M10.1 before any arm starts. Nothing in §0b can be chosen after seeing an arm, so the pre-registration property survives.

**3. `instructions-m10.md:189, 236, 666` + `results/m10_conjunct_arithmetic.json` stress rows — the sequence gates the headline behind the non-headline.**
Quote: "Registered order **C1a → C1b → C2a → C2b** … testing stops at the first non-rejection", while decision 11 (`:161`) makes **C1b** the release gate. The plan's own stress table has `arguana@65%_rest@94%` → avg-6 0.5091 (below C1a's 0.5131 proxy), clean-4 0.5301 (above C1b's 0.5168): **C1b passes, C1a fails, testing stops, no release.** This is not a contrived scenario — arguana is 170-word counter-arguments, the one form with (i) nothing in the M9 pool, (ii) no COV surface at all ("COV still has no argument retrieval"), (iii) assignment to the *generated* half, and (iv) a registered rule (`:537`, A4−A3) that **deletes the generated half from the build** if it does not resolve. The plan's most likely single-dataset failure is the one that kills the sequence at step 1. Symmetrically, `scidocs@65%` reaches C1a and C2a but not C1b, so C2a is unclaimable there too. Fix, before any number exists: register **C1b → C1a → C2b → C2a**. Fixed-sequence FWER control is order-agnostic, the change costs nothing, and it puts the release-critical, M14-registered headline conjunct first. The current order was justified in the feasibility review by "C1b needs more retention than C1a" — an ordering the Codex pass then ruled unestablishable (M1, "a lens, not an ordering").

## MAJOR

**4. `instructions-m10.md:504-506` vs `:420-421` — the 1.9× that justified amendment A7 is only available by breaking the registered mix.**
"**length-bucketed single-chunk batching is part of the build, not an optimisation**" vs "default **75% query-role / 25% document-role** examples **per step**". Queries are ~35 tokens, documents ~230; a homogeneous length bucket is therefore either all-query or all-document. The JSON is explicit: `bs32_mix7525_two_chunks` = **399.6 ex/s**, `bs32_query_bucket_64` = **718**. Keeping the per-step mix costs the whole speedup (139 h, not 81 h) and reverses A7's box-versus-cloud argument; taking the speedup means alternating pure-query and pure-document steps at 3:1, a different trajectory (Adam second moments see two regimes) from the one family B screens. PLANNING §11 already prices the build as separate buckets, so the plan has silently chosen the second — without saying so, and without amending "per step". Fix: state which, and if bucketed, re-word the mix as a presentation share over a window of N steps and register N.

**5. `instructions-m10.md:515-516` — the kill rule cannot fire before the run it would kill has finished.**
"**Kill:** … two consecutive cycle-end evaluations more than 0.0056 below the best." The schedule is three cycles; midpoints are "curve watch only". Two consecutive below-best cycle ends is first possible at the end of cycle 3 = the end of the 200M build. A rented 81–139 GPU-hour run has no reachable abort. This is M9's failure (flat for 1.1B tokens) with a rule that looks like it addresses it. Fix: evaluate the kill on midpoint checkpoints too, or add cycle-internal evaluation points and define the kill on them.

**6. `instructions-m10.md:471-477` — "the plateau rule" is undefined and contradicts the extension rule.**
"**If the plateau rule fires** while the dev→six forecast is below the release bar, exactly one registered top-up cycle runs". M10 defines no plateau rule; the only stopping arithmetic is the extension rule at `:507-514` (`m_k − max(m₁…m_{k−1}) ≥ 0.003` to continue). Read literally, the plateau response fires exactly when the extension rule says to stop, and then runs a cycle "counted against `max_extension_cycles`". Two rules, one event, opposite instructions, no definition. Fix: one sentence — "the plateau rule fires when the extension condition fails", and state that the top-up cycle pre-empts the stop exactly once.

**7. `instructions-m10.md:236` — the release claim is the only one without system-level framing.**
"C1a, C1b pass, C2a fails | release | none; **'beats bge-small on both partitions'**". §Goal (`:223`) applies the whole-system discipline to C2 alone; §Unimpeachable item 6 says "no claim isolates nano from its document tower". But bge-small in `results/perquery.json` is a **symmetric 384-d system with its own index** (`FINAL_MATRIX.md:37`: 66.7 MB query asset, its own document tower). C1 is therefore a 1024-d-index system versus a 384-d-index system — ~2.7× index bytes and a different document-encode cost — and its permitted sentence is the one bare model-vs-model phrase in the plan, and the one that will land on the model card. Fix: bind C1's wording as C2's is, and require index bytes and document-encode cost beside it. This is a frontier paper; dropping the cost axis for the comparator is the one thing it cannot do.

**8. `research/m7-data-licensing.md:80` vs `m10/COV_CANDIDATES.md` — the largest COV family is excluded by the project's own rule.**
"| **Excluded for contamination, licence irrelevant** | S2ORC …, **StackExchange (FiQA)** | **Still OUT of training AND validation.**" (`:79` also puts "StackExchange new downloads (no-LLM-training clickwrap)" fully out for either use.) BRIGHT's queries are StackExchange posts from six sites and it is admitted as one of four COV families — with a three-family floor. Line 53 has the narrow form ("StackExchange **finance** / Reddit finance"); line 80 generalised it and nobody reconciled. Either restore "finance" at `:80` with a note that non-finance StackExchange is admissible for validation under the dataset-level standard, or BRIGHT and CQADupStack both fall and COV drops to two families. Do it before admission, not after a reviewer finds it.

**9. `instructions-m10.md:539` + `:544-551` — the 0.025/14 bound is read from the 36th of 20,000 bootstrap draws.**
0.025/14 = 0.0017857; at B = 20,000 that is order statistic 35.7. The Monte-Carlo sd of the tail count is 6.0 — a ±17% swing in the rank the decision reads, on a surface where most contrasts are already expected to sit near the boundary. `scripts/clean4_bars.py:41` also uses `np.quantile(..., q)` (linear interpolation) where `m9/FINAL_LOCK.md:85` registers `method="inverted_cdf"`. Fix: B = 200,000 for screen contrasts (vectorised, seconds) and pin the quantile method to `inverted_cdf` everywhere.

**10. `instructions-m10.md:528-531`, `:537` — family A, the family with the STOP rule, is exempted from A6 and still transfers by assumption.**
A6's stated purpose is that "no verdict transfers to the build student by assumption", but the order is A → F, so the thesis contrast A3−A2, the A4−A3 drop rule and the *stop-the-milestone* outcome are all decided on bge-small; if F then selects MiniLM-L6 (the default), every one of them transfers to a different student by exactly the assumption A6 removed. Fix: either run F first (its arms need only the A4 corpus, which A also needs), or state in the lock that family A's verdicts are registered as bge-small verdicts and name what would invalidate them.

**11. `instructions-m10.md:596-606` — `arxiv-title`'s one action is not well-posed in either direction.**
"2,000 held-out arXiv titles → **their own abstract** among 100K … if A3−A2 on `arxiv-title` does not resolve … the harvested paper-title and claim forms are dropped from the build". Title→own-abstract is near-lexical known-item retrieval: the teacher will sit near ceiling and a pretrained bge-small backbone will too, so A3−A2 can fail to reach MDE 0.0056 from a **floor effect** and drop the plan's central headline-partition data addition for a reason unrelated to the hypothesis. If instead it always resolves, it only shows that training on arXiv titles helps arXiv-title retrieval. And it tests the wrong relation: scidocs is title→**cited papers'** abstracts, not title→own abstract. Fix: report it, give it no action (as CUREv1 got), and let A3−A2 on the COV macro carry the decision — or replace the gold with a same-category / co-citation relation. Also unresolved: `:537` requires "corrected lower bound > MDE" for family A while `:606` says "same rule and bound as the contrasts" (point ≥ MDE, LB > 0) — two different thresholds for the same decision.

**12. `m10/PLANNING.md` §5–§6 are superseded but a live rule reads them.**
§Recipe (`:510`): "`max_extension_cycles`, an integer the lock fixes from the approved dollars minus **every mandatory line of PLANNING §6**". §6 as written is the withdrawn all-cloud plan: 47 h of confirmations (now ≈17), 12 h of encodes (now on the box), 100 h build (now 40–81), a 2 h mining line for a pass amendment A1 deleted, decision 8 at 100 h (withdrawn), "The box path is withdrawn". §5 still says 3.0M synthetic, 4.5M texts, D-KL3/D-KL1/D-NCE, "14 arms". `max_extension_cycles` computed from it is wrong by tens of GPU-hours. Fix: repoint the rule at the mandate's §Compute table and mark §5–§6 as superseded evidence.
