# M17–M19 post-mortem — 2026-09-13

Cross-milestone review requested by Dylan after M19 closed. Read-only: milestone `STATUS`,
`FINDINGS`, `LEDGER`, `REVIEW` and `PLANNING` files, `instructions-m17/18/19.md`, the M19 split
seal, judgment/audit policies and sensitivity amendment, `results/m19_development_sensitivity.json`,
`ROADMAP.md`, `README.md`, `HARNESS.md`, `run_checks.sh` and git history. Test suites were re-run.
No protected surface, no reserved qrels, no M18 confirmation payload, no repo-wide content search.

This reopens no decision and changes no registered number. It records why three consecutive
milestones produced no encoder improvement and what would change that.

## Findings

### F1 — the measuring instrument was never validated before the experiment was spent on it

Three times, in three different ways:

- **M17.** `training.decision_protocol.eligibility` said "vs v1" in words but named no file and no
  numbers. At decision time the screen had to borrow
  `results/m7_dev_audit_full.json` `per_component_unrounded["p35w-2m-s2500|int8|pool-sqrt"]` under a
  disclosure. The reference for a pass/fail gate was chosen after the observations existed.
- **M18.** The development task (issue/PR prose to one maintainer answer span) had a Stella ceiling
  of 0.1366 nDCG@10 and 0.2320 Recall@10. For roughly three quarters of development queries no
  route placed the labelled answer in the top ten of 79,269 units. The registered margins
  (+0.005 dense, +0.010 fused) sat inside that noise, and development versus confirmation inverted
  the BM25/dense ordering on the same protocol.
- **M19.** The primary gate is +0.03 short-context Precision@10, macro over 12 terms × 3 queries —
  36 queries. One label flip moves a term by 0.0333. The realized attainable range was 0.058 wide,
  about twice the decision threshold.

All three were computable before the run, from the qrels and the metric definition alone.

**Exit.** Register an instrument check as a hard gate before any arm: teacher/ceiling headroom on
the actual qrels, the metric's granularity per single label, and the minimum detectable effect
against the proposed margin. If MDE is at or above the margin, redesign rather than execute.

### F2 — M17's inversion was never diagnosed, and the programme routed around it twice

Five complete 4,000-step schedules, train and held-out losses falling monotonically, retrieval
quality falling with them, same loader, same manifest, same fold-and-quantize call as V0. The
ledger correctly calls it an undiagnosed failure rather than evidence against the method, names the
distinguishing measurement (arm C at steps 500/1000/2000) and two cheaper non-read checks — then
M17 closes.

M18's response was to make the failure mode structurally unavailable (freeze inherited rows, train
only new ones). M19 removed the optimizer entirely. Both are sound risk reductions. Neither answers
the question, which is still open and silently blocks any future vocabulary work: does this
distillation objective move a pooled table in the right direction at all?

**Exit.** Before anything reopens v1.1, run the two checks that need no read allocation: evaluate
the registered objective on the V0 table itself, and compare the cached teacher query targets
against Stella's live query encoding on a sample.

### F3 — the acceptance test was known, named twice, and never made a decision input

The motivating defect is short and bare jargon (`s3`, `k8s`); the stated acceptance test is
LGTM@10. M18's development queries were authored prose. The only instrument aimed at the real
defect — the 21-term bare-term probe — was report-only by construction, and it is the one that
produced the wanted result: for `k8s`, released v1 returns `release v0.8.0` and `v0.8.2`, while the
trained table lifts `Data persistence for k8s deployment` from rank four to rank two.

`m18/REVIEW.md` P1/P2 predicted this before the run and proposed the exit. It was recorded and the
run proceeded. The 30–50 real user queries stayed optional in M18 and voluntary in M19, and were
never collected.

Related provenance gap: `research/andrey-use-case-profile-2026-09-12.md`, cited at
`m18/REVIEW.md:9` as the source record for the requirement, does not exist on disk and has never
existed in git history. The requirement document behind three milestones is not in the evidence
base.

**Exit.** Make the fixed-term side-by-side top ten a registered decision input rather than an
impression, and collect the real query slice before the next protocol seals.

### F4 — model-authored, model-judged, model-audited, with correlated judges

M18: Astra adjudicated 344 prospective pairs down to 140 clear. M19: queries were authored from
pinned corpus evidence, Astra judged all 1,376 pool items, and a second Astra session audited 925
concealed repeats. Primary judge and independent auditor are the same model under different
identities (`astra-m19-development-primary-01`, `astra-m19-development-auditor-01`). The rule that
did hold — a query author may not be its sole relevance judge — is the weaker of the two
independence properties.

**Exit.** Acceptable for a feasibility call, but the claim must carry it: state "model-judged
labels, correlated auditor" wherever the number appears, and treat any agreement statistic as an
upper bound.

### F5 — the judgment schema had no `unsure` outcome, so an honest judge halted the run

The protocol admitted `0`, `1` and `unjudgeable`, and defined `unjudgeable` as a stop condition.
60 of 925 audit items (4.4 %) came back unjudgeable — unremarkable for relevance judging — and that
discarded 1,376 completed judgments. The gate detected a missing category in its own schema, not a
defect in the candidate, the corpus or the pool.

The owner-authorized sensitivity pass then recovered the answer correctly, by exact per-query
enumeration rather than the invalid all-zero/all-one shortcut: dense nDCG@10 is positive under
every assignment (+0.0249 to +0.0925) and dense Precision@10 is positive under every assignment
(+0.0167 to +0.0750). The direction was never in doubt; only the threshold was.

**Exit.** Every future judgment protocol registers an explicit `unsure` label, a scoring rule for
it, and an abstention *rate* above which the pool rather than the run is considered defective.

### F6 — governance consumed most of the execution and the review loops did not converge

Classifying commit subjects by whether they lock, freeze, bind, authenticate, register or record a
review outcome: M17 67 %, M18 54 %, M19 70 %.

M19 ran three implementation review cycles in which each round found new issues in code written to
close the previous round's issues; the loop ended by being declared non-binding (`691fd55`),
followed by two further end-to-end `NO-GO`s. The artifact count is the symptom: four roster-lock
versions, three execution-scope JSONs, four rehearsal results, and v1/v2 pairs of the serving
benchmark, serving receipt, pool pilot and term inventory.

Already addressed by the `CLAUDE.md` governance budget in `6759c3a`. Recorded here as the evidence
that earned it.

### F7 — scaffolding was sized for a production transaction, not an internal proof of concept

M19 built an authenticated one-shot confirmation transaction, separated synthetic and production
trust roots, canonically anchored production inputs and authenticated teacher/row/serving receipts.
Confirmation was never read; its 30-query split is still sealed and unspent. None of that machinery
produced a number.

Also covered by `6759c3a`. The proportionality rule is the right response.

## What went right — preserve these

- **M19 stopped at a pre-registered gate** instead of resolving 60 blocking items while looking at
  the blocker. It is the first milestone in this sequence to refuse a measurement rather than ship
  a weak one.
- **M18's inherited-row freezing** removed M17's failure mode with one gradient mask.
- **M19's engineering result is real and reusable.** Worst int8 bare-term cosine `0.9999541`, max
  coordinate error `0.0005364`, `no_match_encoder_max_abs` exactly `0.0`, released-loader parity
  `2.24e-8`, encoder median/p95 `0.9580`/`1.0370` and end-to-end `0.9956`/`0.9696`. A query
  containing no roster term is bit-identical to released v1: the blast radius is provably zero.
- **Negative results were kept** with their reopening conditions and, for M17, the exact measurement
  that would overturn them.
- **The M18 protected-search incident (E26) was self-reported** and converted into a standing rule
  rather than buried.

## The free experiment still available

Both M19 candidate bundles are built and gated. A twelve-term side-by-side of released v1 against
T0-teacher costs one retrieval pass per model, needs no qrels, judgments or amendment, and carries
the same report-only evidentiary status as M18's spent 21-term probe. `m19/REVIEW.md` names it as
the check that should precede any decision about how much the metric needs to settle; the owner
disposition addressed J1–J4 without picking it up, and no result file for it exists. Given the
`k8s` precedent it is the cheapest available evidence about whether the deterministic row
construction justifies another milestone.
