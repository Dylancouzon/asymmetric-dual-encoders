## 1. Retention levers (the binding constraint)

Our failure is diagnosed: we beat BM25 on single-hop NQ-style queries and lose on multi-hop
HotpotQA and duplicate-question CQADupStack. A normalised bag of token vectors cannot express word
order or composition.

| lever | reported evidence | query cost | absorbable into the table? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Query-side centering / top-PC removal (SIF, uSIF)** | Arora et al. ICLR'17 (SIF) +10-30% STS over plain averaging; potion's own recipe layers Zipf weighting + PCA + SIF and gains **+2.39 MTEB-Retrieval** (32.67 -> 35.06) | one subtract | **NO — genuine new capacity** (see algebra below) |
| **Whitening of the table** | WhiteningBERT (arXiv 2103.15316) **+4.80 Spearman** applying whitening to *mean-pooled* BERT tokens — structurally our exact setup. Isotropy paper (arXiv 2209.00218): RepBERT, also mean-pooled, **+6.9-22.8% nDCG@10 in-domain, up to +25.0% OOD** | one matmul | **YES, pure whitening is absorbable** — only the centering part is new |
| **N-gram / phrase rows** (Sent2Vec, arXiv 1703.02507) | Establishes n-gram rows work; **no BEIR-era numbers exist**. Confirmed: neither Model2Vec/potion nor sentence-transformers static-retrieval use n-grams — pure subword lookup + mean | one extra lookup per n-gram | NO — structurally new |
| Dimension-wise max pooling | **No ablation exists** isolating mean-vs-max on a *dense* bag arm. LightRetriever's amax is on its SPARSE arm and is an inherited SPLADE convention, not evidence | same | NO |
| Aggretriever agg* (arXiv 2208.00511) | Evaluated on 5 of our 6 sets; beats mean-pool/CLS baselines, **magnitude unverified** (PDF unreadable) | avg + projection | NO |
| MRL truncation | "To MRL or not to MRL" (arXiv 2605.16608): non-MRL embeddings are robust to truncation **except beyond ~70% reduction** — so 1024->256 (75%) sits exactly where degradation is expected. No BGE truncation curve exists | — | — |

**The algebra that matters** (do this before believing or dismissing any transform):
- `normalize(W . mean(rows)) == normalize(mean(W . rows))` -> **pure whitening is a
  reparametrisation of the table we already optimise. No new capacity.**
- `q . (A d) = (A^T q) . d` -> **a doc-side linear map is likewise absorbable.**
- `q . (d - mu) = q.d - q.mu` -> a per-query constant -> **document centering cannot change
  ranking at all.**
- But the centering offset `-mu` on the QUERY side is not expressible as a mean of row vectors
  (the always-present [CLS]/[SEP] rows contribute `1/|Q|`, not a constant), so **query-side
  centering / top-PC removal IS new capacity.** This is the cheapest untried lever.

## 2. Contrastive training: my configuration was wrong, not the method

- **Learning rate was 30-300x too high.** Every published frozen-tower / query-only recipe:
  NV-Retriever 1e-5, DAFT 1e-5, BGE/E5/GTE sweeps 1e-5 to 5e-5. We used **3e-3**.
- **There is an analytic result for our exact symptom.** Jing, Vincent, LeCun & Tian (arXiv
  2110.09348) show a large learning rate induces a shift in the embedding mean via negative-pair
  comparisons, driving **dimensional collapse** — loss falling smoothly while the representation
  degenerates. All-embeddings-equal is a provable zero-gradient point of any cosine InfoNCE.
- **My temperature hypothesis was refuted.** 0.01-0.02 is standard (BGE uses 0.01; SamToNe 0.01 on
  asymmetric towers). Wang & Liu (arXiv 2012.09740): low temperature makes the loss *more*
  hardness-aware — it argues for keeping it low. Do not spend budget re-tuning temperature.
- **The false-negative filter is too aggressive.** NV-Retriever (arXiv 2407.15831) tunes to 0.05
  absolute and states larger margins "end up penalising model accuracy"; ours is 0.02, tighter.
  SimANS (arXiv 2210.11773): the negatives scoring *nearest* the positive are the most
  informative and the first thing a margin filter removes. Switch to TopK-PercPos (95% of the
  positive score) or probabilistic down-weighting instead of hard removal.
- **Collapse in asymmetric/frozen-tower contrastive training is documented**: DAFT (arXiv
  2211.04942) reports collapse to a constant representation from tower distribution mismatch and
  fixes it with align-then-contrast — which is exactly our objective C ordering, so C is the right
  shape and was run with the wrong LR.
- **Our frozen index is the easy case for mining**, not the hard one: ANCE needs periodic
  reindexing because its tower moves; ours never does, so a single exact index over the 6.17M
  frozen doc vectors gives permanently valid hard negatives.
- **If collapse persists after the LR fix**: SamToNe (arXiv 2306.02516) adds same-tower
  (query-query) terms to the denominator, purpose-built for asymmetric-tower collapse, no extra
  hyperparameters.
- **Collapse diagnostics to log**: uniformity loss (Wang & Isola), effective rank / singular-value
  spectrum of a query batch, mean pairwise cosine among query embeddings (rising to 1.0 = collapse),
  per-row Adam step magnitude over the ~500 touched rows, and the surviving-negative count and
  hardness histogram after filtering.
- **Positive counterexample worth reading**: LiT (arXiv 2111.07991) — CLIP-style, image tower
  locked, text tower trained from scratch contrastively, and it *works*. Its temperature is a
  **learned** parameter, which is an untried fix here.

## 3. Teacher ceiling (a direct multiplier we had not exploited)

Calibrating MTEB-Retrieval to our six-set via the one model measured both ways (bge-small:
MTEB-Ret 51.68 -> our 0.5042, ratio 0.976):

| teacher | licence | vocab | dim | MTEB-Ret | our six est | x85% | x88% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bge-base-en-v1.5 (current) | MIT | 30,522 | 768 | 53.25 | 0.520 | 0.442 | 0.457 |
| bge-large-en-v1.5 | MIT | 30,522 | 1024 | 54.29 | 0.530 | 0.450 | 0.466 |
| **NovaSearch/stella_en_400M_v5** | **MIT** | **30,528** | 1024 (MRL 512-8192) | **58.97** | **0.575** | **0.489** | **0.506** |

- **stella clears the Tier-1 aim (0.4868) at 85% retention with no fusion at all.** Same small
  vocab, so the table stays 62.5 MB fp16 / 31.3 MB int8. MIT. NovaSearch ships no vector product.
- **Provenance caveat requiring Dylan's judgement**: stella is initialised from Alibaba's
  `gte-large-en-v1.5` weights and released by a small community team. Legally MIT; but Alibaba
  ships OpenSearch Vector Search / AnalyticDB, so the *lineage* touches a competing vendor even
  though the releasing party does not. bge-large is the conservative fallback (+1.04 MTEB-Ret).
- **Expect retention to FALL somewhat as the teacher strengthens** — capacity-gap literature
  (TAKD; DIST arXiv 2205.10536; Distillation Scaling Laws arXiv 2502.08606; Stanton & Izmailov
  NeurIPS'21). DIST specifically shows naive KL matching against a stronger teacher can *degrade*
  the student, recoverable with correlation-preserving losses. So `teacher x retention` is
  directionally right but retention must be re-measured per teacher, never assumed.
- No BAAI English v2/v3 dense successor exists (BGE-M3 is multilingual; its 250K vocab alone
  disqualifies it: 512 MB table > the 466 MB LightRetriever table we exist to beat).
- No evidence that a larger tokenizer vocab helps a bag-of-tokens encoder; potion (30K) beats
  GloVe (840K).

## 4. Zero-query-compute sparse landscape (for the fusion arm)

- `opensearch-neural-sparse-doc-v3-gte` remains SOTA (BEIR-13 0.546); **no v4 exists**. Our
  measured 0.4868 on the six reconstructs to ~0.4855 from its card — consistent.
- **Fusing with it is disqualified twice over** — vendor rule (OpenSearch Service is a competing
  product) and circularity (Tier 1 is *defined* as beating it). Recorded in `m7/EXPLORED.md`.
- **Li-LSR (arXiv 2505.01452): BEIR-13 0.488** with a learned per-token *scalar* table at zero
  query cost, beating SPLADE-v3-Doc (0.470). No weights released; reimplementable from
  `s_i = log(1 + ReLU(w^T E(tok_i) + b))`. This is the shape our own sparse arm would take — but
  note its doc tower is co-trained, so it does not fit "frozen off-the-shelf".
- **Fusion gain depends on regime**: +3.5 to +4.9 nDCG when the dense arm is weaker but
  complementary; it *hurts* when dense is already strong (GTE-Qwen2 -2.0, ReasonIR -2.9). We are
  in the favourable regime. Our own M4 evidence agrees: LR hybrid gained +2.8 over LR dense.
- SPLADE-v3-Doc reconstructs to ~0.4437 on our six — below BM25 on our measurement, so not a
  useful arm.
- **BM42's exclusion is stronger than recorded**: independent reproductions (Reimers, Bergum)
  failed to beat BM25 and Qdrant downgraded it to experimental. **miniCOIL may not be
  zero-query-compute at all** (possible jina-embeddings-v2-small forward pass per query) —
  single unverified source, but it contradicts our notes and matters internally to Qdrant.
  **Verify against FastEmbed source before citing either way.**
